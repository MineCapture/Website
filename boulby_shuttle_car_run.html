Boulby Shuttle Car Run

Drive the shuttle car in a simple two-roadway layout. The continuous miner is cutting in the left-hand heading. Position behind the miner to fill the shuttle car to 20 tonnes, then drive through the cross-cut into the right-hand roadway and park beside the feeder breaker to bank the load.

You have 60 seconds. Crashing damages the shuttle car and slows it for 5 seconds. If you crash while carrying tonnes, some of the load spills out.

Controls: Up/W accelerate, Down/S reverse/brake, Left/A and Right/D steer, P pauses.
