Welcome to Run the Pit, a Mine Management Training Simulation presented by MineCapture in association with S.H.A.F.T. — Strategic Hiring And Future Talent.

This simulator is designed for future and aspiring Mine Managers to test their mettle in a realistic, high-paced production environment. You will need to stay agile and adaptable while dealing with the many challenges of sophisticated underground mining operations, demonstrate excellent people skills and conflict resolution with empathy and understanding for your workforce, and deliver against clear, achievable and entirely realistic KPIs set by the Superior Leadership Team.

Completion of this training does not guarantee competence, promotion, additional pay, or operational control. All references and characters included in this training are purely fictional, and any likeness to real life is purely coincidental or accidental.

Message from Ms Management

Job Opportunity Notice — Closing Date Tomorrow, High Noon

Dear Hopeful Mine Manager,

ManySalts Mine is looking for a manager to begin trial mining of the ManySalts deposit. As this is the first deposit of its kind anywhere in the world, the selection process has naturally been extremely thorough.

Given that you live within 10 miles of the mine, your father and brother already work here, and your lack of relevant mining or management experience, we believe you would be willing to accept a salary significantly below the going market rate and all of the accountability normally avoided by senior leadership.

The Superior Leadership Team requires you to mine a 10,000 tonne bulk sample at a minimum grade of 14% Goldilocks Grade for product testing, agronomy trials, and continued investor optimism.

You may choose your own crack team from our impressive range of candidates and will have access to the finest equipment HOPE Mining has to offer, subject to availability, condition, budget, maintenance priorities, and whether anyone remembered where it was parked. HOPE: it works.

Superior Leadership guarantees this area is strictly for product-trial purposes and that no further mining will ever occur here. As such, there are no meaningful restrictions on mine design, layout, pillar size, support standards, or other traditional obstacles to progress.

The starting salary is £18,500, reviewed annually in line with business needs, personal resilience, and whether payroll notices.

Do you wish to accept this role?
